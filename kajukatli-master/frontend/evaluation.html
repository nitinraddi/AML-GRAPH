Known limits

Read these results in context.

HI-Small is synthetic data with generated suspicious patterns. This prototype has not been validated on real bank transactions. Two benchmark typologies are not covered, and several covered typologies have low or zero attempt recall.

PROTOCOL

Separate fitting from the final check.

Model settings and the alert cutoff are selected using train and validation data. Evasion checks use validation only. The saved final report is guarded against repeat execution or a changed frozen hash.

LIMITS

Coverage and uncertainty still need work.

The current page includes Wilson intervals for attempt recall. Pairwise-difference intervals, baseline clustering variants, and the straddler list are not included yet. Some benchmark typologies are explicitly marked not covered.
